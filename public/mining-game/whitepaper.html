Introduction

Mining game is a Free 2 play, Play 2 Earn and Learn 2 Earn crypto mining simulator game centered around the history of cryptocurrencies, replacing power-hungry mining hardware with NFTs.


WATT v1 (old WATT contracts)

The Crypto Mining Game’s v1 contract address to the emissions token contract on Polygon is WATT v1, which had its genesis block on May 1st, 2022 when 300,000 WATT were minted into existence from the Mining Game Deployer contract; WATT v1 is the contract on Altcoinchain. The Mining Game Started as an idea for the world to learn about mining cryptocurrency using the Proof of Work consensus, only without the requirements of costly computer hardware and electricity.

WATT v2 (current WATT contracts)
WATT v2 on Polygon and WATT v2 on Altcoinchain are the current contracts for the 3D interactive mining game. WATT v1 can be purchased and migrated for WATT v2 at the WATT v1 to V2 migration dApp. The current contract has crosschain swap features where tokens are taken out of circulation on one chain when bridged to another chain, and WATT is also used as energy in mining with NFT mining rig configurations in the mining game while mining real hybrid PoW/PoS blockchains through validators nodes that act as a stratum mining pool which pays out to miners on chain.

Project Overview

The Mining Game Features 3D designed NFT art, representing computer hardware components with varying performance specs written into the code of their contract, which earn WATT when staked, with a halving of earnings to occur every 365 days. Mining Game hardware NFTs may be farmed within a mining rig configuration contract while using WATT to power the rigs at a daily rate according to their configuration in exchange for payouts in the Mining Game’s PoW coins, according to their virtual hardware input. WATT tokens used in mining are burned to the burn address (0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD) when earnings are claimed from the mining contract. When their WATT balance for a mining rig reaches 0, the mining rig configuration will no longer earn PoW coins as if it were powered off. The miner may purchase more WATT and deposit it into the mining contract with their rig configuration to keep the rig mining or they may select to stake some or all of their hardware to earn more WATT, which they may claim daily. Alternatively, the miner could choose to host a mining pool on a local machine or VPS, which will enable their mining rig configuration to consume 0 WATT.

Users with excess WATT may sell to the market or hold for later use or even add it to and remove it from a liquidity pool of their choice. With WATT being at the center of the Mining Game economy, it will help decentralize actual PoW at-home miner's power costs if they also have a stake in The Mining Game. Users could sell their profits and re-invest back into their own costs for hardware mining.

Tokenomics and Economic Model

The mining game started with a FREE MINT GAMING PC NFT and 300,000 WATT minted into supply and a GENESIS BADGE to all testnet junkies. Each NFT component has a variable WATT emission rate for staking and halving every year. New hardware NFTs and mining configurations will be added annually. Miners will earn their share based off of hardware input / network hashrate while their hardware drains WATT from the mining contract which is deposited to the burn address when mining rewards are claimed.

When newer more efficient hardware NFTs are released and used, the network difficulty will increase, making the older versions less profitable per block.

With the ongoing need for miners to burn WATT to run their mining rig configurations, the demand for WATT will increase. However, supply of WATT is only created when NFT hardware components are locked in a staking contract, where the Mining Game Deployer contract will mint new WATT into circulation.

Security Measures

The Mining Game's contracts are secured by both Polygon and Altcoinchain blockchains using the interface of the ERC165 standard. The PoW coin is secured by mining pool operators hosting nodes in exchange for 0 WATT mining costs in the Mining Game and Mining Game NFT miner configurations powered by burning WATT in accordance with NFT mining rig configurations. Effectively 66% of mining rewards for the Mining Game PoW coin will be distributed to miners in the Mining Game with mining rig configurations and 33% will be distributed amongst the mining pools with users hosting nodes connecting their Altcoinchain wallet to the node of the PoW coin will receive 1% of the block reward divided amongst the CPU miners on the network.

This will prevent a 51% attack on the network while also decentralizing both the Altcoinchain blockchain and the Mining Game PoW coin by encouraging users to host nodes and mining pools for a percent of the miner rewards payout with the ability to bridge assets cross-chain within their node or via a decentralized application.

Mining Game pool operators will be required to have 100,000 WATT staked and locked into a DAO contract before they may host their pool and earn their stake in the mining rewards payout. The liquidity pool will mint a new Mining Game DAO token for each 100,000 WATT deposited, in which a contract will split the 33% of the block reward evenly between mining pool hosts via Mining Game Pool-DAO tokens according to their share in the DAO, divided by the amount of the DAO other pool operators hold in the staking pool for their contribution decentralizing mining pools of the blockchain(s).

 *Altcoinchain does not use Infura or Moralis, which record user data such as i.p. and location. All on-chain transactions are transparent.

Gameplay and User Experience

Participants are incentivised for participation within the protocol for staking their NFTs to earn WATT, for running mining rig configurations with their NFTs in exchange for the PoW coin, for hosting nodes to secure both of the blockchains and the option to support the cross-chain bridge mechanism via hosting a node, which has an amount of coin and wrapped token locked into liquidity for real-time bridging between wrapped tokenized versions and native blockchain coins, at a fee set by the holder. The value of tokens in the protocol and the PoW coins produced by miners is purely speculative, depending on supply and demand of the markets.

Roadmap and Future Developments

The original roadmap was published in 2022 as four phases running from Q2 2022 to Q1 2023. Phase 1 shipped essentially in full and Phase 2 in part — the last public version of that page, captured in December 2022, put Phase 1 at 96%, Phase 2 at 10%, Phase 3 at 2% and Phase 4 at 0%. The remaining phases stalled when development paused through 2023 and 2024 following the illness and passing of the project's lead developer. Work resumed in 2025, and everything the original roadmap set out has since been built — in most cases considerably beyond what was scoped. What follows is the original roadmap with its real outcome, and then the work that has landed since. Dates given are the dates the work shipped, not projections.

Community Engagement and Feedback

In an effort to decentralize decision making within the protocol, WATT-DAO will act as the voting mechanism within the Mining Game forum. Users with WATT staked in the DAO will have influence on future community voting power. Within the forum, DAO members may offer suggestions and/or improvements within the Mining Game ecosystem in an effort to further improve decentralization by voting with their stake using DAO tokens. DAO tokens are able to be be minted at a ratio of 100 WATT to 1 WATT-DAO, in which 100 WATT is burned with the WATT-DAO token each time a vote is cast and at a rate of 1,000 WATT for 10 WATT-DAO for the Mining Game Forum proposals.

WATT-DAO proposals may have specific criteria to be met, according to the proposer’s specifications, before voting is finalized.

Within the WATT-DAO forum, stakeholders may provide feedback, which may be upvoted/downvoted for the cost of 0.01 WATT-DAO token. This method will provide value to user feedback in an effort to thwart unnecessary posting or spam within the community.
